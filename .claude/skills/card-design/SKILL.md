# Felt Card Design

## Card back

- Finish: matte, with a fine paper grain and no shine.
- Border: ice white, about 4% of the card height, with a thin inner keyline.
- Monogram: the small S logo, centred in a pressed seal. Its height is 15% to 25% of the card height. Default 20%.
- Use the small logo (`FeltMark`), which reads as an S at every size.
- Corners: radius about 6% of the card height.
- Proportion: 5 wide by 7 high.

## Before you change the back

Show samples first: the full card, the load screen, and the sizes in the app (card entry 70 px, player row 31 px, tab icon 22 px). Wait for the owner to pick.

The picked back goes here once the owner picks it.

## Direction after review (4 Nov 2026)

The owner rejected flat foil. The new direction: ice-white border, geometric blue and black field, monogram on a matte textured back.
At 31 px and under, use a bigger pattern and a 25% monogram, so the pattern stays a pattern.
