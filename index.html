Home game tracker (Texas Hold'em)

Every hand, every buy-in, settled.

Felt records your home game on one phone: buy-ins, hands street by street, and who pays whom when the night ends. It works offline and keeps every figure on your device.

Open Felt Add to iPhone

What it records

Cash games and tournaments

Track in blinds, then set one multiplier to turn units into money. Tournaments keep entries, re-entries, bust order and payouts.

Hands, street by street

Tap to call or fold, type a number to raise, or just count the pot at the end of each street. Action order follows the button.

Split and side pots

All-ins build side pots and return uncalled chips. Run the board up to five times; odd chips go by position.

Who is in the game

A traffic light per player: playing, away with the clock paused, or clocked out. Stack estimates fill in the chip count.

Settle up

Shared expenses and the fewest transfers that square everyone, with a tick for each payment as it lands.

Stats and reports

Standings, VPIP, PFR and showdown rates over any period. Share a game as a PNG report or the full log as CSV.

Your data stays on the phone

There is no account and no server. Games, players and settings live in the phone's own storage. Removing the app from the home screen removes its data too, so back up now and then from Settings, under Data.
